# Economy and progression

## Assets

- `$HOODED` has a fixed supply of 1,000,000,000 tokens at genesis and no future mint authority.
- HOODED Genesis contains exactly 3,000 Hero NFTs: 2,200 Recruit, 600 Specialist, 180 Vanguard, and 20 Icon.
- Primary minting is limited to one per wallet. Secondary ownership still grants membership.
- Mint receipts split 40% burn, 40% seasonal reward funding, and 20% DAO timelock.
- Salary Credits, Reputation, and Renown are indexed game state, not transferable tokens.

## Reward separation

Universal HOODED rewards and regulated Stock Token bonuses use separate manifests, eligibility checks, vaults, and claim screens. The Stock Token path requires identity, jurisdiction, sanctions, wallet-control, and current-eligibility verification. A prohibited claimant gets no Stock Token and no deferred substitute.

The first Stock Token roster is AAPL, NVDA, AMZN, GOOGL, MSFT, and TSLA, subject to canonical Robinhood Chain assets and supported Chainlink feeds being verified before testnet integration.

## Rank ladder

Initiate → Operative → Sentinel → Champion → Paragon → Titan → Luminary → Legend.

Promotion combines Reputation, minimum tenure, and Salary Credit reinvestment. Champion and above have scarce seasonal seats. Legend is challenge-only. Origin tier affects play options and loadout capacity, never direct reward weight.
